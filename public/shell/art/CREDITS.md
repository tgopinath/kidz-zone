# Shell artwork

3D pictures on Home, the Learn/Play/Explore pages and Today's mix: Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D (MIT,
https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), 256 px WebP files taken from the npm package
@lobehub/fluent-emoji-3d@1.1.0 (MIT), renamed by subject. The Money card also uses the RBI specimen
notes and coins in `public/money/currency` (see its CREDITS.md).
