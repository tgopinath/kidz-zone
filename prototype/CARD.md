# KidZ Zone

**Pitch:** Eight children's apps in one, with one profile, one set of stars and one grown-ups area.

**Description:** KidZ Zone brings together Word Wonders, Word Garden, Money Master, Puzzle Quest, Brain Gym, Virtual Pet Universe, Map Explorer and My Supermarket as eight worlds in three groups (Learn, Play, Explore). Each world keeps all its games and levels. A shared layer adds one child profile, a progress summary across worlds, Today's mix (four short activities picked for the child's age), one Settings screen, a play-money jar that links Money and the Shop, and a grown-ups area with a 14-day chart and each world's own dashboard. One calm look (Kolam: Lato, ink on white, one accent per world). Material 3 on Android, Apple HIG on iPhone.
